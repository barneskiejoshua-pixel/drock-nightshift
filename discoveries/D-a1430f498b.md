## Problem

Batch-1 LLM decode is memory-bandwidth bound, so bits per weight set cost; group-wise integer quantization gives every channel in a 128-wide group the same precision and degrades sharply at 3 and 2 bits. BitShare tries to lower bits per weight at equal perplexity without a codebook or fine-tuning.

## Technique

Keep the integer grid and dequantizer (INT4 grid at a 3-bit rate, INT3 at 2 bits). Sort each group's channels by Hessian diagonal into high/middle/low bins that store n, n-1 and n-2 bits; the missing low bits come from one flag bit per group-row (b0 of middle and low codes) and one pair bit per two low-bin weights (b1). A sharing-aware GPTQ chooses every stored value, including the shared bits. BitShare-U uses the U8/D16 layout everywhere (fixed wiring); BitShare-N picks per group from a 44-layout menu, solved as a multiple-choice knapsack by dynamic programming with the constraint that offsets sum to at most zero per layer.

## Foundation

Group-wise asymmetric quantization w_hat = s(q - z); sensitivity = diagonal of H = (2/N) X X^T, which GPTQ already computes; GPTQ second-order error compensation through the Cholesky factor of the inverse Hessian; bit sharing as parity/residue constraints modulo 2 and 4 rather than dropping bits; dynamic programming over layout offsets in multiples of 4. No codebook, no learned quantizer, no permutations stored per weight.

## Compute

Encoder: one GPTQ pass plus an 11-point clipping search x 2 flag values per group-row and a DP with at most 20C/G+1 states; calibration is 128 windows of 2048 tokens from WikiText-2 (SliM-LLM protocol/seed) or the Pile validation split; evaluation on WikiText-2 test and 256 C4 windows. One 48 GB GPU: 70B quantization takes 1.8-1.9 h for GPTQ+clip, 2.2-2.3 h for BitShare-U, 3.1 h for BitShare-N; SliM-LLM's width search costs 43-51 h per model and width. Hardware: synthesizable SystemVerilog decoder, Yosys 0.62 + ABC, sky130_fd_sc_hd tt_025C_1v80, 16 weights/cycle, G=128, 64-bit memory interface.

## Reported

Storage: 3.164 and 2.156 bits/weight in both modes vs 3.148/2.141 for INT3/INT2, i.e. +0.016. SliM-LLM protocol, WikiText-2, 2 bits: BitShare-N 10.11 vs SliM-LLM 17.31 on Llama-2-7B, 6.70 vs 9.47 on 13B, 4.71 vs 5.85 on 70B, 13.29 vs 44.35 on Llama-3-8B, 9.45 vs 12.98 on Llama-3-70B; paper summarizes 19-70% below SliM-LLM and 12.5-35.3% below GPTAQ (abstract: up to 70% and up to 35%). Pile calibration, equal storage: Llama-3-8B 2-bit WikiText-2 15.19 (N) and 16.35 (U) vs 39.51 for GPTQ+clip; Qwen2.5-0.5B 58.02 vs 129.9; C4 21.83 vs 75.00. Equal perplexity: at SliM-LLM's 2-bit WikiText-2 perplexity, BitShare-U needs 1.726-1.758 bits/weight instead of 2.141 (13.6-19.4% less; 21.6% or more on C4); at 3 bits savings are 2.1-14.7%, except Llama-3-70B needs 1.7% more. Hardware: decoder area 318,134 um^2 at 3 bits vs 183,286 INT3 and 237,218 SliM-LLM (1.74x/1.34x); 258,311 vs 139,928/188,672 at 2 bits (1.85x/1.37x); logic delay 7.88 vs 6.39/7.36 ns and 7.63 vs 6.15/6.13 ns. 62 RTL simulations verified every output bit against a Python reference. Energy figures (1.2-1.7 pJ/weight with HBM2, 5.8-8.3 pJ/weight with off-package DRAM, 336 MB less traffic per token on Llama-2-7B) are analytical upper bounds, not measured speedups.

## Reproducibility

Not reproducible from released artifacts. The paper gives Algorithm 1, format details in Appendix B and decoder details in Appendix D, and names datasets, window counts, GPUs and SliM-LLM's seed, but no BitShare encoder, weights, RTL or testbench link appears on the arXiv page or in the paper; the only GitHub links are third-party GPTQ, SliM-LLM, GPTAQ and skywater-pdk. Numerical seed values are not reported. Appendix C-B reports seed/order and GPU sensitivity: BitShare-U gives 8.78 on an RTX 6000 Ada vs 10.53 on an RTX A6000 for Llama-2-7B at 2 bits; sd of log perplexity is 0.05-0.06 over five runs, so the U-vs-N gap is within run noise on the two models tested.

## Limitations

No end-to-end GPU speed measurement; the paper calls that future work. Speedups are byte-count bounds assuming weights streamed once per token at batch size one. Decoder area is 1.34-1.37x SliM-LLM and 1.74-1.85x INT-nn; area excludes placement, routing and clock tree, and delay excludes register and interconnect. Encoding is slower than GPTQ+clip. Equal-perplexity comparisons use a one-layout-per-layer configuration with log-linearly interpolated rates. At 3 bits on Llama-3-70B BitShare-U is above SliM-LLM and BitShare-N is 1.1% above it. Scope is post-training scalar integer quantization without gradient-based calibration. 2-bit results depend on GPU rounding. No code or RTL released.
