## Problem

KV-cache memory grows linearly with generated history in long video diffusion transformers and reaches tens of GB; at 2 bits, temporally distant cache vectors have a large range and high quantization error. The paper targets long-video DiT conditioning-cache compression; it does not address any listed DRock problem.

## Technique

Temporal Rotation Quantization (TRQ). At each image patch, split cached K/V into blocks of nB=8 consecutive latent frames, take the block temporal mean mu_B, and fit one orthogonal rotation R_B=G_B*H per block (keys and values separately). G_B is a balanced tree of d-1 2x2 planar rotations with angles from {0, pi/4, pi/2, 3pi/4}, 2 bits each, chosen greedily from leaves to root to concentrate mu_B in one coordinate; H is a fixed normalized Walsh-Hadamard transform that spreads it flat. The asymmetric INT2 quantizer offset then absorbs the near-constant mean. Fit is online per video, no gradients or weight changes; fused Triton kernels do the encode/decode on CUDA.

## Foundation

Translation invariance of asymmetric uniform quantization: a component constant across channels costs no quantization error. Orthogonal G_B preserves L2 norm, so maximizing mass in coordinate 1 minimizes residual ||rho||^2 = ||mu||^2 - d*c^2; Hadamard's first row (1/sqrt(d))*1 maps (sqrt(d)*c,0,...) to c*1. Theorem 1: greedy angle choice is globally optimal over 4^(d-1) assignments for a fixed tree layout under mean-flatness objective J_B (Eq. 15); Corollary 1 bounds reconstruction MSE. Architecture: append-only flow-matching video-DiT continuation cache per patch/head/layer.

## Compute

No training, gradients, or weight changes. Greedy fit costs O(d) using only the block mean; angles cost 2(d-1) bits (254 bits for d=128). Apply and invert use 2(d-1) adds plus fast Walsh-Hadamard d*log2(d) adds, about 1150 adds per vector at d=128. Metadata: FP4 E2M1 minima/scales in 64-channel groups; offline permutation and tree-layout calibration on 8 videos. Reported H100 80GB Triton timings: 1.785 ms per K/V pair and 2.743 s total cache vs 6.638 ms and 10.196 s for native PyTorch; table runtime overhead 1.05x-1.08x over dense. DRock's i5-8400/Arc A380 has no CUDA, so those kernels do not run here.

Reported results, 480p, 100 matched prompts sampled from the 1003-prompt MovieGen Video Bench suite, same prompts across methods, sample standard errors: LongCat-Video-13B subject consistency QVG 90.38+/-0.58 -> TRQ 92.02+/-0.57; PSNR 24.06->26.53, SSIM 0.83->0.87, LPIPS 0.13->0.10; compression 6.88x vs 6.89x BF16; runtime/dense 1.07x vs 1.05x. HY-WorldPlay-8B subject QVG 86.33+/-0.68 -> TRQ 87.61+/-0.74 (KIVI also 87.61+/-0.66); PSNR 21.16->27.50; compression 3.15x vs 3.19x; QVG wins image quality 64.16+/-1.04 vs TRQ 63.44+/-1.09. Paper text also reports +1.0 point subject on Self-Forcing-Wan-1.3B over QVG, best on all 7 metrics for LongCat and Self-Forcing at 480p and 720p, and best or tied on 6 of 7 for HY. Ablation (20 videos, 480p) LongCat subject full 94.21 vs H-only 93.84 vs H+random-2x2 92.12; HY full 88.53 vs 87.89 vs 87.37. ## Modifications

No changes to model weights, training objective, sampling schedule, or attention math beyond applying R_B at encode and R_B^T at decode; it adds per-block 2-bit angle metadata, FP4 E2M1 min/scale metadata, offline permutation/tree-layout calibration, and online angle fitting. Any port into DRock would be new code: a non-Triton CPU/Arc implementation, a non-temporal grouping of Qwen KV rows, local-cache integration, and new tests.

## Limitations

Source-stated: depends on temporal redundancy, so weak content with fast motion or scene cuts; fixed nB=8 in experiments; global optimality holds only for a fixed tree layout, chosen offline; not tried on other model families or modalities; TRQ and centroid methods such as QVG may be complementary. Visible in the tables: HY image quality worse than QVG, HY runtime overhead 1.08x vs QVG 1.06x, and small compression gains (6.89x vs 6.88x; 3.19x vs 3.15x). My assessment: CUDA/Triton only, no code URL, no seeds, modest sample sizes, video-DiT-specific, and it measures generation quality rather than agent correctness.
