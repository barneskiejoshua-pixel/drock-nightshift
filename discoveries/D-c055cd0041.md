## Problem

Multi-tenant agent serving fills GPU KV memory, so programs wait on head-of-line admission while a same-engine summarizer either blocks the agent or competes with it for batch slots.

## Technique

Aggron, a vLLM scheduler subclass, summarizes only the prose tail after the latest non-prose tool observation (file/shell reads, SQL, pytest/python stay verbatim; reasoning, tool calls, and web results are prose). A per-session counter K counts that tail. A queue-position threshold theta runs from Tmin=1024 at the back to just under Tdef=4096 at the head (w=0.5, epsilon=64). A separate summarization queue runs only when the agentic head does not fit in free KV. Three gates must pass: predicted issue-to-landing (p90 of the last 50 summaries, cold-start formula for the first 10) finishes before admission; K >= 2.5 * summary cap; the rewrite frees at least one KV block. The summary is frozen and appended, not re-summarized; a rare full reset folds everything, including non-prose, near the window limit.

## Foundation

Continuous batching with paged KV admission (vLLM/Orca): under load, agentic latency is mostly queue wait because HBM fills before compute. The controller is a position-dependent threshold plus a latency predictor, not a new model.

## Compute

Accuracy on one NVIDIA H100 NVL (94 GB HBM3), vLLM 0.24, 131072-token window, 256 sequences, 92% of HBM as KV (~2.49M tokens, 1056-token blocks). Primary model Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8; also GLM-4.7-Flash and Gemma-4-26B-A4B. Serving numbers come from a trace-driven simulator of that engine (1000 programs, Poisson arrivals). Frontier sweep uses TP=8 to 32 on H100s. A side-summarizer arm adds one GPU running a 35B model. Same model does the summaries in the main design.

## Reported

Paper's headline, intro and conclusion, high load vs Slipstream then APEC: P99 program latency -27% and -31%, mean latency -11% and -15%, throughput 1.34x and 1.40x. Section 6.1, simulated Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at lambda=1 req/s: throughput 1.44x vanilla, 1.40x APEC, 1.34x Slipstream; P99 -33%, -31%, -27%. Batch occupancy 26% to 39% of the 256-sequence cap. At lambda=0.2, SWE P99 4.5x lower than vanilla; Terminal-Bench 1.3x; MCPMark 1.6x; DeepResearch Bench matches Slipstream (0.42 h). Accuracy (Table 5): every grow-1k and grow-4k cell is inside that benchmark's run-to-run noise; the intro says within 2.7 pp. Noise is about +/-6.7-9.2 pp on SWE and +/-12-20 pp on Terminal-Bench. Whole-context summarization (Table 1, Qwen): SWE 58% at a 4K trigger vs 64-65% at 32K-120K; DRB stays ~39-42%. A 4K prose cap on production traces cuts HBM 1.35-1.57x; at low load mean latency is 1.02x, near the knee 1.31-1.33x, and at high rate throughput 1.07x. Simulator vs two live vanilla runs (Table 4): at lambda=0.2 every metric within 3.4%; at lambda=0.05 throughput and P99 within 4.3%, median and mean off by up to 16% while host speed varied 21%. Frontier models at lambda=1: up to +14% throughput over a 120K or 24K cap; 5-17% on all four if summaries reuse cached tail KV. DSV4 (3.4 KiB/token) loses 1.7% without reuse. Moving summaries to an extra GPU at a 24K cap loses 26% on DSV4. Separate vs shared summary queue: +1.9-8.0% throughput and -3.8-8.1% P99 on Qwen once summaries fire. Forcing summaries that cannot hide costs up to -3.8% throughput and +47% P99, worst at lambda=0.05.

## Modifications

DRock is one Ollama process on an i5-8400 and an Arc A380 (6 GB), not a multi-tenant vLLM queue, so the summarization queue, admission gates, and H100 block pool have nowhere to sit. The only portable piece is 'summarize prose, keep tool output verbatim,' which would have to replace prompt_budget.py's char-ceiling drop. With no queue to hide in, Aggron itself falls back to a synchronous summary, a second model call on the critical path. The models in the paper do not fit 6 GB.

## Limitations

Gains need a saturated shared KV pool. At no queue, the prose cap is about 1.02x mean latency, and unhidden summaries make the separate queue worse than a synchronous 4K cap (up to +47% P99 at lambda=0.05). Small-KV models can lose throughput (DSV4 -1.7% without tail reuse; side GPU -26% at a 24K cap). Terminal-Bench noise is +/-12-20 pp, so accuracy claims there are unresolved. Simulator mean and median miss live vanilla by up to 16% when the box is not queueing. Summaries still re-prefill the rewritten span unless tail KV is reused. A full-window reset does compress non-prose. No released code.
